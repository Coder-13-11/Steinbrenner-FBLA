Who We Are

Steinbrenner FBLA builds future business leaders

Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) is a nonprofit 501(c)(3) education association with a quarter million students preparing for careers in business and related fields. Steinbrenner FBLA is our local chapter at Steinbrenner High School — meetings, service, competitions, and leadership that actually open doors.

Members compete at district, state, and nationals in 80+ events, log service hours, and build experience for college applications.

Who We Are

Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) is a nonprofit 501(c)(3) education association with over 230,000 students preparing for careers in business and business-related fields. Steinbrenner FBLA is proud to bring that mission to life at Steinbrenner High School in Lutz, Florida.

We compete annually at the district, state, and national levels — representing our school in 80+ business, technology, and leadership events that build real skills judges and colleges notice.

Whether you're passionate about coding, finance, marketing, law, or public speaking — there's an event and a community waiting for you here.
